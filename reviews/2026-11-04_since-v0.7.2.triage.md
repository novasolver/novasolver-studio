# Triage: second opinion on the changes since v0.7.2 (Codex gpt-6.1-sol, 2026-11-04)

Report: `2026-11-04_since-v0.7.2.codex.md`. Each finding was read in the code. All six concern the surrogate builder; Flash had none.

| # | Severity | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Readiness warnings (a load a modal solve ignores) were lost from the model | **fixed**: `_solve` keeps readiness warning codes with the result warnings, so they reach the model and every prediction; the range checks in `build` keep the messages as `setup_warnings`, returned by status and printed from the first response. `test_a_load_a_modal_solve_ignores_is_reported_at_the_start_and_in_every_point` |
| 2 | High | The reported GCI describes the fine mesh, while training uses the case's mesh | **fixed**: each mesh check also records the training mesh's distance from the extrapolated value; the model stores `training_size` and `training_error_max_pct`, and status and predict state it beside the GCI. The training mesh stays as the person set it. `test_mesh_error_is_for_the_mesh_the_model_learned_from` |
| 3 | Medium | Two simultaneous resumes could start two builders | **fixed**: atomic per-study lock with a re-read of the state under it; a lock older than 60 s counts as left by a dead process. `test_simultaneous_resumes_start_one_builder`, `test_a_lock_left_by_a_dead_process_does_not_block_resume` |
| 4 | Medium | Whole-number fields passed the check at both ends and failed on the first design point | **fixed**: `build` also checks a fractional point, so such a field is refused before the study starts. `test_a_whole_number_field_is_refused_before_anything_starts` |
| 5 | Medium | `umin_center` and `umax_outlet` were labelled dimensionless | **fixed**: `NAMED_UNITS`; a test checks every default output has its unit (only `cd` has none) |
| 6 | Low | Unknown output names returned an empty success | **fixed**: `predict` raises `err.surrogate_outputs` with the available names. `test_an_output_it_does_not_have_is_an_error` |

Codex reported no findings on security, UI or packaging. It did not run the solver or the packaged app; the frozen exe's surrogate worker is checked in the 0.7.3 release smoke test.
