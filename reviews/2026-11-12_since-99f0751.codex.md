## High — Heat-connectivity checks disappear on derived problems

**What goes wrong:** On a thermal `20×4×4` bar, with a sink at `x=0` and heat applied at `x=20`, I prescribed a final cut removing the last three layers. The original problem returned `loads_carried=False`; both the symmetry-reduced and refinement-created problems returned `True`. Calling `_finish()` produced `heat_not_carried` on the original problem and no warnings on the half model.

Consequently, thermal runs using symmetry or refinement can present temperatures and conductance for a part that does not carry its heat input.

**Why:** `build()` attaches `carry_nodes` dynamically. Neither `mirror_half()` (`genesis.py:1274`) nor `refine_problem()` (`genesis.py:1430`) propagates it. The fallback at line 790 treats missing provenance as an empty set, skips validation, and leaves `carried=True`. `optimize_symmetric()` then returns that result without checking connectivity on the reconstructed whole model.

**Suggested fix:** Make heat-input provenance an explicit part of `Genesis`. Remap its node mask during symmetry reduction, preserve it during refinement, and validate the reconstructed final part. Add regression tests covering both derived-problem paths.

## Medium — STEP can lose a bridge while passing the new volume check

**What goes wrong:** I reproduced the contour-stage failure using a `30×4×14` prismatic grid: two `8×8` blocks joined by a `10×1` bridge, extruded along Y. Densities were `1` in the blocks, `0.4` in the bridge, and `0.3` elsewhere, with binary threshold `0.35`.

The analysed voxels and new STL each had one connected component. STEP extraction selected contour level `0.5293715563602746` and produced **two separated polygons**, removing the bridge.

**Why:** The new remapping exists only inside `Genesis.surface()`. STEP’s `section()` still calls `coarse_field()`, which retains the old rule raising low-density retained cells only to `0.501`. Global area matching can select a level above that value and erase the member.

This extraction defect already existed in the called code. The new read-back check at `genesis_step.py:183` does not catch it: it compares the written volume against the already disconnected polygons.

**Suggested fix:** Share the corrected extraction field between STL and STEP. Check that contouring and simplification preserve required connections and boundary attachments; reject the STEP export if they do not.
