# Roadmap

What we are working on, in order. It changes as people use Studio; tell us in an issue or a discussion what you need.

## Now

- Linux program package, alongside the Windows one and the Python package.
- Parts from free CAD tools: test STEP files from them, and fix what breaks on import and meshing.
- A larger public benchmark set, re-solved automatically and published with every release.

## Next

- NovaSolver Flash on imported CAD: vary dimensions, not only loads and materials, when you build your own instant model.
- More complex worked examples, each with its error bar shown.
- macOS program package.

## Not planned

- Certification of a design. Studio tells you what it checked; the engineering decision stays with you.
- Sending your models anywhere. Everything runs on your own computer.
